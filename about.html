About our owner

Daniel Lipp

Leadership that combines decades of HVAC ownership with Marine Corps discipline.

Industry tenure

More than 20 years in HVAC. Previously owned and operated Comfort Control Heating and Cooling from 2001 to 2019 before founding Kuebler Mechanical in 2019. Proven track record of high-quality, customer-focused service across residential and commercial systems.

Certifications & safety

EPA Universal certification along with forklift and scissor lift certifications—reflecting dedication to safety, precision, and professional standards on every install.

United States Marine Corps

Daniel’s leadership was shaped through eight years of service, achieving the rank of Staff Sergeant (SSGT), serving aboard the USS Theodore Roosevelt in the Persian Gulf, and earning the Navy Achievement Medal.

This combination of military discipline, technical training, and decades of HVAC experience equips Kuebler Mechanical to handle installations with consistency, reliability, and attention to detail.
