Symptom-led triage

If you’re noticing any of these—don’t wait for a total shutdown

Rattling or banging

Often a loose part—early attention can prevent cascading damage.

Short-cycling

Turning off and on in spurts may point to an electrical issue.

Blowing hot air

Multiple root causes—professional diagnosis beats guesswork.

Frozen coil

Needs immediate attention to help prevent the unit from seizing.

Damp spots or puddles

Leaks under the unit signal a problem that can worsen quickly.

Weak airflow / hot spots

Uneven rooms and weak vents deserve a full system check.
